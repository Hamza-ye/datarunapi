# Product Outcomes And Success Signals

## Outcome 1: Faster Domain Launches

Teams can stand up new domain workflows by composing shared platform capabilities rather than building bespoke systems.

Success signals:
- Time to first usable workflow decreases over successive domains.
- Reuse of shared capabilities increases release over release.
- New domain onboarding requires less custom engineering.

## Outcome 2: Strong Operational Accountability

Every meaningful action remains attributable to actor, context, and time.

Success signals:
- Stakeholders can audit lifecycle decisions without manual reconstruction.
- Review and approval paths are explicit and inspectable.
- Disputes about who did what are resolved from system records.

## Outcome 3: Reliable Completeness And Progress Visibility

Teams can continuously compare expected work against actual completion.

Success signals:
- Missed or late work is visible early enough to intervene.
- Progress can be tracked by period, scope, and responsible actor.
- Supervisors can act on risk before cycle end.

## Outcome 4: Safe Product Evolution

New capabilities can be added without destabilizing existing operational flows.

Success signals:
- Existing workflows remain stable while new ones are introduced.
- Domain additions do not require redesign of shared foundations.
- Product roadmap expands breadth without architectural churn.

## Outcome 5: Cross-Domain Usability

Different domains can use the same platform language for identity, capture, progress, and history.

Success signals:
- Reporting and oversight patterns remain recognizable across domains.
- Teams transfer operational practices with minimal retraining.
- Common product surfaces serve multiple domain contexts.

## Planning Guidance

When planning proposals are generated, prioritize outcomes in this order:
1. Operational accountability
2. Completeness and progress visibility
3. Safe evolution
4. Faster domain launches
5. Cross-domain usability

Rationale: if trust and visibility are weak, speed gains are not durable.
